# From Soil to Silicon

## Film overview

- English title: From Soil to Silicon
- Amharic title: ከአፈር ወደ ቴክኖሎጂ
- Genre: Autobiographical short film
- Target duration: 83 seconds
- Narration language: Amharic
- Subtitle language: English
- Formats: 9:16 and 16:9

## Story

The film follows a child growing up in a small farming
village in Ethiopia. From the age of seven, he works
alongside his mother, manually removing weeds and
harvesting crops during school breaks.

Despite the difficulties, his mother's sacrifices allow
him to pursue education. At university, he discovers
computer science and artificial intelligence.

He begins to imagine how intelligent agricultural systems
could reduce the exhausting manual labor he experienced
throughout his childhood.

The film ends with his ambition to use technology to
give back to the farming community where his journey began.

## Audience

Primary audience:
- Ethiopian farming families
- Young Ethiopian students
- Family members and relatives

Secondary audience:
- Ethiopian diaspora
- Engineers interested in agricultural technology

## Design intent

The audience should understand the sacrifices behind
educational opportunities and feel hopeful about the
potential of technology to improve farmers' lives.

The film should inspire rather than simply evoke sympathy.

## Emotional progression

1. Nostalgia and hardship
2. Determination and sacrifice
3. Discovery and possibility
4. Hope and responsibility

## Visual direction

Cinematic realism with natural lighting and culturally
appropriate Ethiopian rural environments.

Childhood scenes use warm earthy colors.
Education scenes use more neutral colors.
Future scenes use vibrant greens and warm sunlight.

Characters are fictionalized representations rather than
recognizable reproductions of actual family members.

## Music direction

An original instrumental score inspired by Ethiopian
traditional instrumentation, including gentle krar and
masenqo textures.

The music gradually transitions from reflective and
melancholic to hopeful and uplifting.

Narration must remain intelligible throughout the film.

## Design constraints

- Ten shots and a three-second AI disclosure end card.
- Gentle movements to reduce video generation artifacts.
- Consistent character references across relevant shots.
- Center-safe composition for horizontal and vertical crops.
- Agricultural robots are presented as an imagined future,
  not as existing achievements.
- Avoid displaying sensitive family information.
