# Ethics, Consent and Licensing

## Story provenance

The film is inspired by the creator's real childhood
experiences growing up in a farming community in Ethiopia.

## Character representation

All generated characters will be fictionalized visual
representations of the people described in the story.

No personal photographs or biometric references of real
family members will be supplied to generation models
without their informed consent.

The narration describes personal experiences but uses
a synthetic, prebuilt narrator voice.

No real person's voice will be cloned.

## Consent record

No recognizable likenesses or cloned voices of real
people are currently planned.

If this changes, informed consent must be obtained and
recorded before generating or publishing those assets.

## Dramatization

Some scenes reconstruct childhood experiences for
storytelling purposes.

Future agricultural technology scenes are illustrative
and do not represent systems already built or deployed.

## AI disclosure

Both exported films will include a visible end card
stating: "Made with AI".

AI-generated content disclosure will be enabled on the
publishing platform where applicable.

Existing provenance metadata and watermarks will not
be intentionally removed.

will not be sent to external providers unnecessarily.
